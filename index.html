Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and investing in real estate

You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score, and a down payment saved, and you plan to stay in the area for at least a few years. Our team can help assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage pre-approval services.

Spring and early summer are traditionally the strongest selling seasons, since buyers are more active and homes show better in good weather. That said, local market conditions and your own timeline matter more than the calendar, so we'll help you find the right moment for your situation.

Your offer should be grounded in comparable sales, the home's condition, and current market conditions. In competitive markets you may need to offer at or above asking price. Our agents provide a detailed comparative market analysis to help you offer competitively without overpaying.

Closing costs typically run a few percent of the purchase price and cover loan origination, appraisal, title insurance, and taxes among other fees. Both buyers and sellers have costs, and they vary by location and can often be negotiated. We'll walk you through a detailed breakdown early in the process.

Yes, always. A professional inspection is a modest cost that can save you from far larger surprises down the line. We recommend one for every property type and can connect you with qualified, licensed inspectors in your area.

For buyers, closing typically takes four to six weeks after an offer is accepted, though finding the right home can take longer. For sellers, our properties typically go under contract within a month. We'll give you a realistic timeline based on your situation and local market.

We pair modern tools like virtual tours and market analytics with a dedicated agent for every client. Our high client satisfaction rate and fast average selling times reflect a team that stays hands-on from the first search to closing day.
